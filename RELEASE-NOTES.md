## 1.0 — 2026-10-04

Journey Recorder 1.0 is here!

Record a user journey in any app — taps, text input, scrolls and screen changes — then review it step by step and export it as Markdown, ready to turn into test automation. Passwords, PINs, OTPs and card numbers are masked before anything is saved, and you can redact any typed value before export. A floating bubble lets you pause or stop anytime. Your journeys stay on your device until you export them.
