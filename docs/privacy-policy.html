Privacy Policy

Journey Recorder · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Journey Recorder (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a software-QA tool that records a user journey you walk through in another app — the taps, text input, and screen changes — so you can export it as a Markdown step list for test automation. This policy explains what the app accesses, what little it collects, and how that information is handled.

The short version: Your recordings stay on your device. The app only captures a journey while you explicitly start a recording, masks passwords and sensitive fields before they are ever saved, and never uploads or stores your recordings on any server. Recordings leave your device only when you choose to export or share them. We collect only anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to fix bugs and improve the app.

1. How the app captures journeys

Journey Recorder uses Android’s Accessibility Service to observe the app you choose to record. While a recording is active, it reads interaction events and on-screen element details — the control you tapped, its identifiers or visible text, screen titles, and text you type — in order to reconstruct the steps you performed.

  • Nothing is captured unless you start a recording and pick a target app. When no recording is running, the service observes nothing.
  • Only the app you selected is recorded. Journey Recorder’s own screens are always excluded.
  • The accessibility service is used solely to capture journeys. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app’s recording feature.

2. How recordings are stored

Recorded journeys are saved only in a private database on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

  • Sensitive text is masked before it is saved. Passwords and fields that look sensitive (PIN, OTP, CVV, card numbers) are detected and masked in the capture pipeline before anything is written to storage — the raw value is never persisted.
  • You can review every captured step in the app and redact any typed value before exporting.
  • Recordings leave your device only when you deliberately export them — via the Android share sheet or by saving a Markdown file to your Downloads. You control where an exported file goes.
  • Deleting a journey in the app removes it from your device.

3. Information we collect

We collect a limited amount of non-personal, anonymous information through Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to detect problems. This does not include your recordings, the content of any captured step, the text you type, screen contents, or the identity of the app you recorded.

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • Which screens are viewed and which actions are performed in Journey Recorder (for example, that a recording was started, stopped, or discarded, that an export happened, or that a value was redacted), and aggregate counts such as the number of steps in a recording.
  • A Google-generated, app-specific installation identifier, plus general device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, country).

Crash and diagnostic reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • If the app crashes, we receive a diagnostic report: the error and stack trace, device state, OS and app version. This helps us fix the problem.

4. Permissions we request and why

  • Accessibility Service — required. Used to observe the app you choose to record and capture the steps of your journey. Enabled by you in your device’s accessibility settings; you can turn it off at any time.
  • Display over other apps — optional. Used for the floating control bubble that lets you pause or stop a recording. The bubble normally uses the accessibility overlay and needs no extra permission.
  • Notifications — used to show an ongoing notification while recording, so you can stop even if the bubble is dismissed.
  • Vibrate — used for haptic feedback on the control bubble.
  • Internet / network state — used only to send the anonymous diagnostics described above. Your recordings are never transmitted over it.

The app has no account, no sign-up, and shows no ads.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the app’s core feature (recording, reviewing, and exporting on-device journeys).
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve them.
  • To identify, diagnose, and fix crashes and bugs.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your data and we do not share your recordings with anyone. The anonymous analytics and crash data described above are processed by Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics) acting as our service provider. Their handling of that data is governed by Google’s privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase & Privacy

7. Data retention

Your recordings remain on your device and under your control; the app keeps no separate copy. Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google’s standard retention periods.

8. Security

Because your recordings never leave your device, they are protected by your device’s own security, and sensitive fields are masked before they are stored. Analytics and crash data are transmitted to Firebase over encrypted connections.

9. Your choices

  • You can turn the capture service off at any time in your device’s Settings → Accessibility → Journey Recorder.
  • You can review and redact any captured value before exporting, and delete any journey from within the app.
  • You can stop all analytics and crash collection by uninstalling the app.
  • You decide if and when a recording leaves your device, by choosing to export or share it.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy
